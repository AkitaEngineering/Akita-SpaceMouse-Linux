## v1.0.1 — 2026-11-22

- Licensed the Community Edition under GPLv3.
- Added contact information for paid setup, fleet deployment, and support.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-22

First official Akita Engineering release.

- One-command setup for systemd-based Arch, Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, and compatible distributions.
- Onshape launcher and local bridge with complete event reads and clean handling of daemon disconnects.
- Live diagnostics and tuning for sensitivity, dead zone, and axis inversion.
- Separate SpacePilot Pro and SpaceMouse Pro button profiles with editable assignments.
- Uninstall preview, optional profile removal, and restoration of the distro driver after an upstream install.
- Browser setup for Onshape, Layerling, and Tinkercad.
