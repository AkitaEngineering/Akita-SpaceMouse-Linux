# Paid setup, fleet deployment, and support

Akita Engineering sells remote setup, fleet deployment, and support for [Akita SpaceMouse for Linux](README.md). The Community Edition stays free under the [GNU General Public License version 3](LICENSE). Install and run it on as many compatible Linux machines as you like. The installer uses public downloads and runs without an Akita account or licence key.

The fee pays for Akita's work and, on the monthly plan, a response commitment. The software remains GPL-3.0-only. You can keep using, copying, and sharing the Community Edition under that license.

This page is the published price list. It becomes a contract only when Akita confirms the work, price, and tax in a written quote and you accept that quote.

Akita Engineering invoices from Niagara Falls, Ontario, Canada. These rates are for business customers. Prices exclude tax. The list was set on 22 November 2026.

### Remote setup

One workstation and up to 45 minutes of remote work. Akita installs the Community Edition and the Linux driver, completes Onshape setup in one browser profile, and sets motion tuning and a button profile for the connected device.

Have a systemd desktop, an internet connection, sudo access, and the device plugged in before the session. Time past 45 minutes uses the hourly rate.

### On-demand troubleshooting

Remote help with the driver, the browser setup, or device input for this software. The booked time includes diagnosis and follow-up on that issue. Hourly work has no response-time target. A monthly support plan uses the target below.

### Fleet pilot

Up to five workstations that share one Linux distribution and one browser. Akita delivers a verified Onshape setup on that configuration, one shared button profile, and a rollout and rollback guide.

### Assisted rollout

After the pilot, each additional workstation that matches the piloted Linux distribution and browser. The guide is yours to follow, so the remaining machines can be installed without Akita.

A Canadian team with 25 matching workstations pays C$649 for the pilot and C$49 for each of the other 20 machines: **C$1,629 before tax**. The same count is US$1,279 in the United States and €1,229 in the European Union.

### Monthly team support

Month to month, for up to ten workstations. The plan includes one pooled hour each month for setup, driver, browser, and input issues on this software. Akita sends an initial response on the next business day, Monday to Friday, America/Toronto, excluding Ontario statutory holidays. Unused time ends with the month. Add workstations or hours at the rates above. The plan renews on the invoice date until you cancel. Cancel in writing before that date.

## Tax

The quote states the tax Akita will add.

- **Canada.** GST/HST is added when it applies.
- **United States.** State and local sales tax is added when it applies.
- **European Union businesses.** The quote is in euros. Akita confirms the VAT treatment from the buyer's VAT details. Cross-border services supplied from Canada to an EU business are commonly invoiced under the reverse-charge rule.
- **European Union consumers.** Ask for a separate quote. That quote includes the tax and the contract information required for a consumer sale.

## Outside this price list

These services are remote. On-site work, a second Linux or browser configuration, and fleets above the published bands are quoted separately.

The fee covers Akita's work on Akita SpaceMouse for Linux. Hardware, an Onshape subscription, and general Linux administration sit outside it.

A proprietary or white-label licence is not part of this offer. The Onshape bridge in this release uses the GPLv3 [spacenav-ws](https://github.com/RmStorm/spacenav-ws) project, so this release stays under GPL-3.0-only. A later product that Akita can license on its own would have its own agreement.
